Decision tool · Conversion A/B

Editorial vs Retail vs Hybrid — pick the conversion winner.

Three identical surfaces (PLP card grid · PDP buy box · HOF editorial hero) shown three ways. A = Playfair on product names (current editorial). B = DM Sans on names (clean retail). C = DM Sans on names + one amber word per headline (hybrid — keeps VK's color signature without going full Playfair). Body, prices, and buttons stay identical in all three.

Locked rules across all three — Pricing & numbers always DM Sans 500. Buttons always DM Sans 600 uppercase. Eyebrows always DM Sans uppercase 12px. The only things that move are the product name and the amber accent word.

Variant A — Editorial (current)

Playfair Display on product names + section titles. DM Sans on body, prices, buttons. Brand voice = pro-audio heritage, story-led, "this gear has history."

Variant B — Retail (clean)

DM Sans on product names + body + prices + buttons. Playfair only on H1 / hero / HOF article titles. Brand voice = clean, fast, scannable, conversion-led.

Variant C — Hybrid (recommended)

DM Sans on names + body + prices + buttons (B's clarity) + amber word injection on one key word per name / hero. Keeps the VK color signature without going Playfair on every product.

How to decide — quick rule of thumb

Variant A (Editorial · Playfair on names): what you have today. Stronger differentiation, pro-audio heritage feel, closer to Apple / NYT product pages. Risk: feels "magaziney" on PLP and slows scan-speed.

Variant B (Retail · DM Sans on names): Sweetwater / Reverb / Thomann pattern. Easier to scan, faster decisions, better for paid traffic. Risk: loses the VK character — looks like every other gear store.

Variant C (Hybrid · DM Sans + amber word injection — AJ's recommendation): DM Sans clarity for scan-speed + one amber word per name to keep VK's color signature. Playfair stays in true editorial moments only (HOF article H1, About hero, program landing H1, homepage hero). Pricing & numbers always DM Sans 500. Best of both.
