CUSTOM · BUFFET COMPOSITION

Custom — pick your own

Use when
The page is one-of-a-kind and doesn't fit any locked template. Still required: nav → hero → strip → body → CTA → footer. The body is your call. Pull from the buffets.
Recipe:
1. Pick 1 hero (heroes catalog · H1–H9)
2. Pick 1 strip (strip picker · V7 / V10 / V8 / V12 / V11)
3. Stack 3–6 body sections (secondary or PLP body buffet)
4. End with 1 CTA section (B-G email form or B-H related gear)
5. Footer always last

Rule of one: max one dark section per page (hero OR stat block OR spotlight — not all three).
