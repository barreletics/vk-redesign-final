Buffet · Secondary templates

5 secondary templates. Pick by purpose.

Every secondary page (anything that isn't homepage / category PLP / PDP / cart / checkout) maps to one of these 5 templates. Each template lists the live built examples to copy from.

T1 · Editorial Light

Editorial light — image-led marketing landing.

Light hero (#EDE8E2), short headline, big body image, 3-column proof, CTA / form section, value strip, footer. The default template for any new marketing landing page that doesn't fit T2-T5.

Use whenYou're launching a new program / collection landing that needs to convert visitors quickly and doesn't have long-form content.

T2 · Deep Article

Deep article — long-form heritage / Hall of Fame.

Dark editorial hero (H3), long body with pull-quotes, sidebar TOC, related-gear shop CTA at end. The template for every gear heritage piece, Hall of Fame inductee, or technical guide.

Use whenYou're writing a 2,000-3,000 word story-led page where authority and SEO matter more than fast conversion.

T3 · Program / Service

Program / service — 3-step process + pricing.

Light hero, credential strip (V7 / V10), 3-step process module, pricing transparency block, FAQ, CTA. Optimized for users who already know what they want and need to qualify.

Use whenYou're describing a paid program, service, or financing offer where the visitor needs to understand how it works and what it costs before converting.

T4 · Promo / Drop

Promo / drop — time-bound campaign landing.

Photo-overlay hero (H4) with countdown / drop tag, 2x2 editorial grid, urgency-led CTA, "while it lasts" inventory cue, value strip. The template for limited-time promotions, holiday drops, and Open Box waves.

Use whenYou have a time-bound offer or limited inventory drop and the page needs urgency cues + photo-led hero.

T5 · Policy / Long-form

Policy / long-form — utility, no hero photo.

Centered light header (H2), 2-column TOC + body, anchor-linked sections, contact-us footer block. The template for every policy, terms, shipping, returns, or support page. Conversion is not the goal — clarity and SEO are.

Use whenYou're writing a policy / terms / FAQ / support / contact page where users come with a specific question and need to find the answer fast.
